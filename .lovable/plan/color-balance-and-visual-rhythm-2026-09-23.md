# Color balance and visual rhythm

## Goal
Rebalance customer-facing pages from continuous gender-color canvases into alternating strong color, white/off-white, and soft-tint sections while preserving the men/women identity and all workflows.

## Scope to inspect
- Home, About, How It Works
- Gender and treatment category pages
- Treatment and lab-panel product details
- Lab Testing and BloodPanels
- Shared theme tokens and reusable section styles

## Guardrails
- Presentation-only changes; no product data, clinical, payment, eligibility, filtering, or navigation behavior changes.
- Preserve the sticky white header, mobile layout, footer branding, gender context, and back-navigation behavior.
- Verify contrast, section rhythm, overflow, and responsiveness at all requested widths.
